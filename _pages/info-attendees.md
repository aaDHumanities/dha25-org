Parents are welcome to breastfeed across ANU campus and must not be discriminated against if they choose to do so. Employees and students also have access to parenting rooms across campus. These rooms offer additional privacy for breastfeeding and provide a space to express or change babies.

## Transport and Travel Information 

### Public Transport 

Transport Canberra runs a unified network of buses and light rail, making it easy to travel to major spots throughout the city. The light rail connects Gungahlin to the city centre, with stops at Dickson and Mitchell, and operates every 5 to 15 minutes. Suburbs are linked to town centres and other key locations through both local and rapid bus services. For up-to-date schedules and journey planning, check the Transport Canberra website. 

To use public transport, you can pay with a MyWay+ card, which offers fare discounts and daily caps. These cards can be purchased and recharged online, at ticket machines, and other outlets. Alternatively, single tickets are available for purchase from bus drivers or at light rail stations. 

### Taxis and Rideshare 

Canberra has multiple taxi providers offering service across the city: 

- Cabxpress – 02 6260 6011

Rideshare platforms like Uber and Ola are also available for convenient travel options. 

### Cycling and Walking 

Known for its extensive paths, Canberra is ideal for cycling and walking. The University of Canberra connects to the city’s network of trails, and bike racks and storage facilities are widely available. For maps and route details, visit the cycling section of the Transport Canberra website. 

## Tourism

For tailored help and more information, stop by the Canberra and Region Visitors Centre at Regatta Point. The centre provides maps, brochures, and local advice to enhance your visit. Bike hire is also available for those wanting to explore the city on two wheels.
